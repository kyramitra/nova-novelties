About the restaurant

Global comfort food with a cosmic point of view.

Nova Novelties is Kyra Mitra's imagined restaurant where the menu travels from creamy masala tea to Sunday roast, sushi, biryani, and mango sticky rice. The food is familiar, the setting is starry, and every table gets a little sparkle.
